# Student Guide: Failure Analysis of Bolted Joint (EC-05)

## Overview
This engineering challenge puts you in the role of a Mechanical Design Engineer investigating the failure of a bolted structural joint. You will apply concepts from Design of Machine Elements and Strength of Materials.

## Preparation
1. Review Unit I of SPPU PCC303-MEC (Design Considerations, Materials, Factor of Safety, Stress Concentration).
2. Keep a calculator or spreadsheet handy for engineering calculations.

## Tips for Success
- Identify components accurately based on standard engineering conventions.
- Ensure consistent units when performing stress and force calculations (e.g., converting kN to N, maintaining mm for area).
- Base your final engineering decision purely on the calculated factor of safety and observable stress concentration risks.
- Write your final report professionally, justifying every claim with evidence.
