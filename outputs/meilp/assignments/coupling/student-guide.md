# Student Guide — Assignment 09 (EC-09 / EA-09)
## Identification and Selection of Couplings Used in Mechanical Power Transmission

### Welcome to Engineering Challenge EA-09
In this assignment, you act as a **Senior Mechanical Design Engineer** at **Kirloskar Pump Systems, Pune**. You are tasked with analyzing the power transmission system connecting a 22 kW electric motor to an industrial centrifugal pump and selecting the most suitable coupling element to ensure safe, reliable, and smooth continuous operation.

---

### Learning Objectives
1. Understand the working principles and mechanical functions of couplings in power transmission lines.
2. Identify key mechanical components on 3D Engineering Asset **EA-09**.
3. Classify couplings into Rigid and Flexible categories and analyze their application limits.
4. Compare coupling performance across rigidity, misalignment capacity, shock damping, maintenance ease, and cost.
5. Calculate transmitted nominal torque $T$ and design torque $T_d$ for given operating conditions.
6. Apply engineering judgement to select the optimal coupling and defend your choice with evidence-based technical justification.

---

### Assignment Progression (Activities 1 to 12)

- **Activity 1 — Project Charter**: Review the industrial scenario, system parameters, and deliverables. Accept the project charter.
- **Activity 2 — Engineering Context**: Examine why two shafts cannot be made continuous and explore shaft connection mechanics.
- **Activity 3 — Component Identification**: Identify 10 key mechanical components on Engineering Asset **EA-09**.
- **Activity 4 — Working Principle**: Explain the torque transmission path and answer the working principle MCQ.
- **Activity 5 — Classification of Couplings**: Classify rigid and flexible coupling types according to SPPU DME Unit I standards.
- **Activity 6 — Application Analysis**: Match industrial drive applications (Motor-Pump, Compressor, Conveyor, Machine Tool) to their performance requirements.
- **Activity 7 — Coupling Comparison**: Compare coupling performance across rigidity, shock absorption, maintenance, and cost.
- **Activity 8 — Engineering Requirements & Calculation**: Calculate transmitted nominal torque $T$ and design torque $T_d$ ($P=22\text{ kW}, N=1440\text{ rpm}, K_l=1.5$).
- **Activity 9 — Coupling Selection**: Select the most appropriate coupling for the motor-pump system.
- **Activity 10 — Engineering Justification**: Formulate engineering justification supported by torque capacity, misalignment accommodation, and shock damping.
- **Activity 11 — Engineering Decision**: Finalize the decision matrix and document why alternative couplings were rejected.
- **Activity 12 — Engineering Report & Reflection**: Synthesize your findings and complete technical reflections.
- **Submission**: Review your score breakdown and submit your final work.
