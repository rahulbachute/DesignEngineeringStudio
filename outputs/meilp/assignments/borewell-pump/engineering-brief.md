# Project Charter: Ergonomic Design and Safety Verification of a Borewell Pump Hand Lever

## 1. Industry Scenario

You are working as a Mechanical Design Engineer in a company manufacturing manually operated borewell pumps. The company has received field complaints that operators experience excessive operating effort, fatigue, discomfort, and poor ergonomics during prolonged use. 

Management has tasked the Design Team to investigate whether the existing hand lever design is satisfactory. You must evaluate the existing design and determine whether it satisfies both **Structural Safety** and **Ergonomic Requirements**. Finally, recommend an improved design wherever necessary. 

## 2. Project Objectives

- Understand the forces acting on the borewell pump hand lever (a first-class lever).
- Estimate the Required Human Effort and compare it against ergonomic limits.
- Calculate the Maximum Bending Moment and Maximum Bending Stress on the lever cross-section.
- Evaluate the Factor of Safety using the selected material's yield strength.
- Conclude whether the design is safe for regular operation without causing structural failure or operator fatigue.

## 3. Engineering Deliverables

You are expected to produce an Engineering Notebook containing:
- Hand lever component identification and working principle explanation.
- Design requirements and engineering assumptions.
- Free Body Diagram showing all loads and reactions.
- Step-by-step engineering calculations for effort, bending stress, and factor of safety.
- A final engineering decision based on empirical evidence and calculations.
- Recommendations for design improvement.

## 4. Academic Alignment
This assignment is aligned with SPPU PCC303-MEC Design of Machine Elements (2024 Pattern), Unit-I. It integrates Machine Design, Engineering Mechanics, Ergonomics, and Material Selection while developing critical engineering judgement.
