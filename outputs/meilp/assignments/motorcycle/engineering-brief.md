# Engineering Brief

## Project Charter

Urban Mobility Service Workshop wants to review the stability and basic load capacity of motorcycle side stands used on commuter motorcycles.

## Engineering Background

A side stand supports a parked motorcycle at an inclined angle. Stability depends on geometry, center of gravity projection, tire contact points, and the stand foot contact point.

## Learning Outcomes

- Identify major side stand elements.
- Explain stability using center of gravity and support polygon reasoning.
- Compare material choices for a support member.
- Estimate side stand load for preliminary design.

## Engineering Activities

Students identify components, explain stability, recommend material, estimate load, and prepare an engineering notebook.

## Report / Engineering Notebook

The notebook should include observations, assumptions, calculation steps, design notes, and conclusion.
