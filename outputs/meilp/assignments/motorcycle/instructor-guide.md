# Instructor Guide

## Objectives

- Introduce stability reasoning for a simple mechanical support.
- Connect geometry, loading, and material selection.
- Encourage structured engineering documentation.

## Expected Answers

Students should identify the pivot bracket, stand leg, return spring, and foot pad. Stability explanations should mention center of gravity projection and the support polygon. Load estimation should use motorcycle weight and the configured load share.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing total vehicle weight with side stand load.
- Ignoring lean angle and contact geometry.
- Recommending a material without considering fabrication and strength.

## Discussion Points

- How ground slope affects stability.
- Why a return spring is needed.
- Why the foot pad area matters on soft ground.

## Suggested Evaluation

Use `rubric.json`. Award marks for reasoning clarity and correct calculation setup.
