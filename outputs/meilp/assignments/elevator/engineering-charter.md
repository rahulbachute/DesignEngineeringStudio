# EC-01 Engineering Charter

## Project Code

EC-01

## Project Title

Safety Verification of Elevator Suspension Cables

## Client

ABC Commercial Complex Pvt Ltd

## Project Background

ABC Commercial Complex Pvt Ltd is installing a new passenger elevator at Hinjewadi, Pune. Before procurement, management requires an independent engineering review of the selected suspension cables.

## Engineering Context

Students act as mechanical design engineers. The task is to understand the traction elevator system, identify major components, evaluate candidate cable material, estimate load per rope, and prepare an engineering decision.

## Objectives

- Interpret the project charter and safety-critical context.
- Identify major traction elevator components.
- Explain the working principle of a traction elevator.
- Select a suitable suspension cable material.
- Calculate passenger load, total load, and load per rope.
- Recommend approve, reject, or redesign with engineering justification.

## Deliverables

- Component identification response
- Working principle explanation
- Material selection recommendation
- Engineering calculations
- Engineering decision
- Report / engineering notebook
- Reflection responses

## Professional Responsibility

Elevator suspension systems are safety-critical. A professional engineer must prioritize public safety, code compliance, certified materials, reliable inspection, and transparent documentation.

## Safety Considerations

This academic challenge does not replace certified elevator design. Real procurement requires applicable standards, manufacturer data, dynamic loading analysis, factor of safety, inspection records, and qualified professional approval.

## Acceptance Criteria

The project is complete when all configured activities are attempted, the 12-mark CCE rubric can be applied, and the student summary documents CO, PO, PSO, Bloom level, and reflection evidence.
