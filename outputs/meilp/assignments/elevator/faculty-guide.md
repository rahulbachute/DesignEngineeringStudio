# EC-01 Faculty Guide

## Objectives

- Support SPPU 2024 Pattern continuous assessment.
- Provide OBE evidence for CO1.
- Map student responses to PO and PSO contribution.
- Evaluate engineering judgment in a safety-critical mechanical design context.

## Expected Answers

- Component identification should include traction motor, traction sheave, counterweight, steel wire rope, guide rail, elevator car, controller, and safety gear.
- Working principle should explain motor-driven sheave traction, rope movement, car-counterweight balance, guided motion, controller coordination, and safety gear function.
- Material selection should usually recommend certified high-carbon steel wire rope, while noting corrosion, certification, factor of safety, and inspection requirements.
- Calculations should show passenger load = 10 x 75 = 750 kg, total static load = 750 + 700 = 1450 kg, and static load per rope = 1450 / 4 = 362.5 kg before factor of safety and dynamic effects.
- Engineering decision should justify approve, reject, or redesign based on material suitability, safety, load distribution, and missing procurement evidence.

## Common Mistakes

- Ignoring car weight.
- Dividing passenger load only by four ropes.
- Treating cost as more important than safety.
- Recommending approval without factor-of-safety discussion.
- Writing reflection without linking to CO1.

## Discussion Points

- Why elevators require high factors of safety.
- Static load versus dynamic operating load.
- Role of certified wire rope.
- Importance of safety gear and inspection.
- Ethical responsibility in public-use machinery.

Use `rubric.json`. Record marks, CO attainment, PO contribution, PSO contribution, Bloom level achievement, and faculty remarks using `evaluation-template.json`.
