# Engineering Project Brief

## Client Background

ABC Commercial Complex Pvt Ltd is developing a commercial facility at Hinjewadi, Pune. The building requires a passenger elevator for daily public and staff movement.

## Machine Description

The proposed system is a traction elevator. A traction motor drives a sheave, and steel wire ropes suspend and move the elevator car with help from a counterweight. Guide rails maintain alignment, while the controller coordinates motion and safety functions.

## Problem Statement

Before procurement, management requires an independent engineering review of the selected suspension cables. Students act as mechanical design engineers and verify whether the proposed suspension approach is reasonable from a safety and load-sharing perspective.

## Scope

- Identify major elevator components.
- Explain the working principle of a traction elevator.
- Compare candidate suspension materials.
- Rank key design considerations.
- Calculate passenger load, total supported load, and load per rope.
- Prepare a final engineering recommendation.

## Deliverables

- Completed activity responses.
- Cable load calculations.
- Material recommendation.
- Final approve, reject, or redesign decision with justification.

## Professional Responsibility

Elevator systems are safety-critical. Engineering recommendations must prioritize public safety, reliability, code compliance, and clear documentation.
