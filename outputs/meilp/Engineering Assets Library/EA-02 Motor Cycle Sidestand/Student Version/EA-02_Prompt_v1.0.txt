Design Engineering Studio (DES)
Engineering Asset EA-02
Motorcycle Side Stand Assembly (Student Version)
You are an expert technical illustrator specializing in Mechanical Engineering education.
Create a clean, textbook-quality engineering illustration of a Motorcycle Side Stand Assembly for a Design Engineering Studio (DES) challenge in Design of Machine Elements.
The illustration is intended for student learning and assessment, where students will identify the components of the side stand assembly.
The illustration must resemble an engineering drawing found in standard Mechanical Engineering textbooks.
1. DIAGRAM TYPE
Generate a 2D engineering technical illustration (side view).
Style requirements:


Vector-style drawing

Engineering textbook appearance

Uniform line thickness

No perspective

No 3D rendering

No gradients

No shadows

No textures

White background
2. ENGINEERING CONTEXT
Illustrate a motorcycle side stand in deployed (parking) position supporting the motorcycle.
Include only the lower portion of the motorcycle required to understand the side stand mechanism.
The illustration should clearly show the relationship between the motorcycle frame, pivot mechanism, side stand, spring, and ground.
The side stand should appear in a realistic parked position.
3. COMPONENTS TO BE SHOWN
Illustrate the following engineering components.
Do NOT write their names on the drawing.
Instead, identify each component using numbered orange circular callouts connected by clean leader lines.
The numbered callouts should correspond to:


Motorcycle Frame

Side Stand Mounting Bracket

Pivot Pin

Side Stand Arm

Return Spring

Spring Hook

Foot Pad

Ground Contact Point

Ground Line
The numbers must be clearly visible.
Leader lines should never overlap or intersect.
4. VISUAL STYLE
Use:


Dark navy engineering line colour (#1A2B5C)

Orange numbered callout circles (#F28C28)

White number inside each circle

Thin leader lines

White background

Clean engineering proportions

No dimensions

No hidden lines

No centre lines

No section views

No labels other than the numbered callouts
The appearance should resemble engineering figures used in undergraduate laboratory manuals.
5. LAYOUT
The drawing should occupy approximately 70% of the canvas.
Maintain generous white space around the illustration.
Arrange the assembly so that:


Motorcycle frame appears at the upper left.

Mounting bracket is clearly visible.

Pivot pin is easy to identify.

Side stand arm extends diagonally downward.

Return spring is clearly visible between the bracket and stand arm.

Foot pad touches the ground.

Ground line is shown beneath the foot pad.
Leader lines should remain uncluttered and easy to follow.
6. EDUCATIONAL REQUIREMENTS
The illustration should support the following learning activity:

Identify the components of a Motorcycle Side Stand Assembly.
Students should be able to identify each numbered component by observing the illustration without ambiguity.
The diagram should be suitable for:


Image Label Activities

Drag-and-Drop Component Identification

Multiple Choice Questions

Viva Examination

Engineering Observation Exercises
