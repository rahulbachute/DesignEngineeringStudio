# DES Challenge Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before faculty review, before student pilot, and before release.

## Educational Quality

- [ ] The challenge has a clear learning purpose.
- [ ] Activities are sequenced from understanding to application to judgement.
- [ ] Instructions are student-friendly and unambiguous.
- [ ] The challenge promotes reasoning, not only calculation.
- [ ] Reflection is meaningful and connected to the engineering task.

## Engineering Authenticity

- [ ] The scenario resembles a realistic engineering situation.
- [ ] Constraints, assumptions, and inputs are technically credible.
- [ ] The expected solution requires engineering judgement.
- [ ] Safety, reliability, manufacturability, or service conditions are considered where relevant.
- [ ] Final conclusions are supported by evidence.

## Industry Relevance

- [ ] The challenge names a product, system, process, or stakeholder.
- [ ] The task reflects design, verification, inspection, troubleshooting, or decision-making practice.
- [ ] Required data resembles real engineering information.
- [ ] The deliverable is similar to an engineering note, report, recommendation, or review.

## Accessibility

- [ ] Language is clear and concise.
- [ ] Images or diagrams have descriptive captions.
- [ ] Units and symbols are defined.
- [ ] Students can complete the task without hidden assumptions.
- [ ] Required resources are available before the activity begins.

## CCE Alignment

- [ ] CCE marks are stated.
- [ ] Marks are distributed across meaningful evidence.
- [ ] Rubric criteria are observable.
- [ ] Intermediate work and final judgement are both considered.
- [ ] The assessment can be completed within the estimated time.

## OBE Alignment

- [ ] CO mapping is complete.
- [ ] PO mapping is justified with activity evidence.
- [ ] PSO mapping is justified where applicable.
- [ ] Bloom level matches the student task.
- [ ] Graduate attributes are supported by student evidence.

## WK Alignment

- [ ] WK mapping uses only relevant WK codes.
- [ ] Each mapped WK has an evidence statement.
- [ ] WK evidence appears in activities or submission requirements.
- [ ] The rubric evaluates the mapped knowledge.

## JSON Completeness

- [ ] Platform-compatible content files are complete where required.
- [ ] Challenge metadata is consistent across authoring documents and content files.
- [ ] Interactive components named in the challenge are available in the frozen platform.
- [ ] No new schema fields are introduced.
- [ ] No namespace names are changed.

## Grammar

- [ ] Spelling and punctuation are checked.
- [ ] Terminology is consistent.
- [ ] Sentences are short enough for classroom use.
- [ ] Student-facing instructions use direct language.

## Technical Accuracy

- [ ] Equations are correct.
- [ ] Symbols and units are defined.
- [ ] Numerical examples have been checked.
- [ ] Assumptions match the selected model.
- [ ] Expected answers and rubric levels are technically defensible.
