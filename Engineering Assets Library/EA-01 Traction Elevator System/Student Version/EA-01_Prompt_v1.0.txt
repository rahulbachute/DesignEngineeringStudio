TITLE

Engineering System Familiarization

Challenge:
EC-01 – Elevator Suspension Cable Safety Verification

=====================================================================

OBJECTIVE

Create a clean, accurate, engineering textbook style cross-sectional illustration of a modern traction elevator system.

The purpose of this figure is educational.

Students will study the engineering system BEFORE beginning engineering calculations.

The emphasis is understanding the machine element, the load path, and the engineering system.

• No photorealism

• No gradients
• No perspective
• Orthographic cross-sectional view
• Portrait A4 layout
• Publication quality
• Suitable for printing in engineering manuals

ENGINEERING ACCURACY

The illustration should resemble a real traction elevator installation.

Ensure:

• Correct rope routing
• Correct counterweight location
• Proper guide rail arrangement
• Correct drive sheave arrangement
• Realistic proportions
• Proper machine room arrangement
• Correct suspension of the elevator car
• Suspension wire ropes should be visually prominent because they are the primary machine element of this challenge.

=====================================================================

EDUCATIONAL EMPHASIS

The Suspension Wire Rope is the focus of this Engineering Challenge.

Make the ropes slightly thicker than in a standard engineering drawing so students immediately recognize the primary machine element.

The rope should be visually distinguishable while maintaining a professional engineering drawing appearance.

DO NOT write component names.

Use numbered circular callouts (1–20).

Ensure:

• Callouts do not overlap.
• Leader lines are neat.
• Numbers are easy to read.
• The illustration remains uncluttered.

Leave adequate white space around the figure for annotation.

Only include details necessary to understand the engineering system.
