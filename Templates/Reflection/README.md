# Reflection Template

## Reflection Prompts

Answer the prompts after completing the engineering activities.

1. What was the most important engineering concept used in this challenge?
2. Which assumption had the greatest effect on your result?
3. How did the interactive component change or confirm your understanding?
4. What would you check before approving this design in industry?
5. What safety, sustainability, cost, or maintenance issue should be considered?
6. What would you improve if you repeated the challenge?

## Faculty Evaluation Notes

Look for:

- Connection between calculation and physical meaning.
- Awareness of assumptions and limits.
- Evidence-based judgement.
- Clear statement of learning.
