# Student Guide: Borewell Pump Hand Lever

## How to Succeed in this Assignment
1. **Understand the Mechanics:** Before starting calculations, study the provided diagram. Identify the fulcrum, the effort arm, and the load arm. Remember that a manual borewell pump uses a first-class lever mechanism.
2. **Read the Design Requirements:** Make sure you extract all the correct numerical values (e.g. pump load, stroke length) from the Project Charter and input them accurately into the platform.
3. **Ergonomics Matter:** A good engineer designs for the human operator. Evaluate whether the required effort is something a person can do repeatedly without fatigue.
4. **Step-by-Step Calculations:** Perform your calculations in order. Finding the mechanical advantage first will help you determine the required human effort and the reaction forces.
5. **Engineering Justification:** The final "Engineering Decision" is the most critical part of this assignment. Your conclusion (SAFE or UNSAFE) must be supported by the numerical evidence you calculated.
