# EC-01 Student Guide

## Your Role

You are a mechanical design engineer reviewing suspension cable safety for a new passenger elevator.

## How To Complete The Challenge

1. Choose individual or group attempt mode.
2. Read and accept the project charter.
3. Complete each activity in order.
4. Save your draft before moving ahead.
5. Record assumptions and calculations clearly.
6. Submit after reviewing your summary.

## Engineering Notebook

Record:

- Observations
- Assumptions
- Calculations
- Engineering Notes
- Conclusion

## Reflection

Connect your learning to CO1 and explain how the challenge improved your understanding of safe machine element design.
