# Faculty Guide: Failure Analysis of Bolted Joint (EC-05)

## Educational Context
This assignment bridges theoretical calculations with real-world failure analysis. It is designed to evaluate L3 (Apply), L4 (Analyse), and L5 (Evaluate) Bloom's levels.

## Key Concepts Evaluated
- Bolted joint load transfer mechanisms.
- Bolt dimension calculations (Core diameter, Stress area).
- Material property selection (Yield strength comparison).
- Failure mechanisms (Stress concentration, Fatigue vs Overload).
- Engineering recommendation based on quantitative data.

## Assessment Notes
- Component Identification: Ensure students correctly map terms to the visual asset.
- Calculations: Check for correct unit conversions (e.g. Area in mm², Load in N).
- Decision & Report: Strong responses will correlate the localized stress concentration at the thread root/run-out with the calculated margin of safety to justify a redesign.

## Expected Time
The activity is estimated to take students 120 minutes. Evaluators should allocate approximately 5-7 minutes per submission for grading the open-ended reflection sections.
